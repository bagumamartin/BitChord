# BitChord 1.0.6

## Now Playing

- A title too long for the player no longer widens it. On iPhone the player stayed pushed past the right edge, and on Mac the title spilled past the left. Long titles now stay in their column and scroll.

## Updates

- Release notes now cover every version you skipped. The update sheet lists each release between your version and the latest, newest first, and the first launch after an update shows what changed since the version you last ran.
- Release notes are formatted: headings, lists, bold text, links and commands display properly instead of as raw Markdown.
- **Mac:** **Check for Updates…** is in the BitChord menu.
