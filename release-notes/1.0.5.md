# BitChord 1.0.5

## Artist pages

- Artist pages follow the YouTube Music layout: the name over the artwork, then Shuffle, a large Play button and Subscribe.
- Top songs have their own heading. Each shelf shows five items with **Show all**, and About comes last with the description, subscriber count and monthly listeners.
- Every credited artist on a song opens their own page, not only the first. Names without a link are matched exactly, or fall back to a search.

## Now Playing

- The title and artist scroll when they don't fit, with the explicit mark, a star and a More button beside them. Tapping the title opens the album.
- Artwork follows the track that actually loaded, and old embedded artwork is cleared when the track changes.
- The playback source line sits centred in the timestamp row.
- **Mac:** opening the player from a pushed page, such as an artist page, no longer hides the sidebar with no way back. Closing the player returns you to that page.

## Loading screens

- Loading placeholders now have the shape of the page they stand in for: Home shelves, library grids, artist and album pages, Explore tiles and song rows. They no longer jump or resize while they shimmer.

## Playback and lyrics

- When playback fails while you are signed in, BitChord no longer asks you to sign in. A bot check is reported only when YouTube says so for every client; otherwise you get network advice.
- KuGou lyrics must match the track's artist and script, so an English song no longer gets another artist's Korean lyrics.
- Nested TTML markup no longer leaks into lyrics, and XML comments are ignored.
- Sign-in to YouTube Music asks which account to use.

## Mac

- Single-click playback is now a setting (single or double click). The first click on an inactive window works, and typing in search keeps spaces and arrow keys.
- Toasts clear the playback pill, wrap instead of truncating, and stay up long enough to read.
- The compact navigation bar appears only in narrow windows.

## Requirements

- iOS and iPadOS 18 or later. The IPA is unsigned: sign it with your own signing app and identity.
- macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon. The DMG is ad-hoc signed; existing installs update through Sparkle.
