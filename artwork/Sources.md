# BitChord Apple cover

## Design assets used

- Original app icon: the user’s Downloads folder, October 1, 2026. iPad and iPhone screenshots: the user’s Downloads folder, October 25, 2026. Mac screenshot: a full-screen capture from the user’s MacBook Air, November 1, 2026. The profile photo is covered by a plain circle on all three devices.
- Mac frame: Apple’s MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display, 16-inch, Space Black, from the Product Bezels on https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/ (`Bezel-MacBook-Pro-PROLED-XDR-Display.dmg`, accepted under the Apple Design Resources License, November 1, 2026). The screenshot is scaled to fill the bezel’s screen, trimming 13 px from its sides.
- iPhone frame: Hoverify’s independently drawn iPhone 17 Pro Max, Deep Blue. https://tryhoverify.com/tools/device-frames/iphone-17-pro-max/
- iPad frame: Hoverify’s independently drawn iPad Pro 11 M5, Space Black, rotated into landscape. https://tryhoverify.com/tools/device-frames/ipad-pro-11-m5/

Hoverify permits these frames in personal and commercial mockups. Its frame files may not be redistributed as a frame pack or mockup tool. Keep them as assets for this cover. https://tryhoverify.com/tools/device-frames/

## Editable structure

`BitChord-Apple-Cover.sketch` contains editable text, native gradient lights, the rounded icon mask, separate iPad and iPhone screenshot and bezel layers, and the Mac as one flattened screenshot-in-bezel image. Feature text sits directly on the background, without a container. Device screenshots retain their original aspect ratios. The artboard crops the foreground devices at the bottom, following the supplied reference’s overlapping composition.

The Apple Design Resources License permits the bezel only in depictions of mock-ups for apps that run solely on Apple platforms, and forbids distributing it on its own. The Sketch file therefore holds a single flattened image of the screenshot inside the bezel, not a separate bezel layer, and the bezel PNG itself is not in this repository. Anyone reusing this cover must keep to those terms. The iPad and iPhone keep the independently drawn Hoverify frames.

## Pinterest pin

The second artboard in `BitChord-Apple-Cover.sketch`, "BitChord · Pinterest pin", is a 2:3 (1000 × 1500) version of the cover built from the same layers: the headline is larger, the devices are scaled and stacked under it, and the footer line reads "Free and open source · Hi-Res audio · Automix". It is exported as `cover-exports/BitChord-Pinterest-1000x1500.png` and `cover-exports/BitChord-Pinterest-2000x3000.png`. The Mac stays the same flattened screenshot-in-bezel image, so the Apple Design Resources License terms above apply unchanged.
